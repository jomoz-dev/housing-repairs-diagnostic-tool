# Business Requirements Document

## Project Background

Housing organisations receive repair requests covering a wide range of property issues.

Different staff members may assess requests differently, resulting in inconsistent prioritisation and response times.

A tool is required to help standardise the assessment process.

## Problem Statement

Repair categorisation and prioritisation can be inconsistent when relying solely on manual decision making.

Managers also require easy access to reporting information and historical records.

## Objectives

- Standardise repair assessment decisions.
- Improve prioritisation consistency.
- Support operational reporting.
- Enable historical record retrieval.
- Support management decision making.

## Stakeholders

- Housing Officers
- Repairs Teams
- Repairs Managers
- Housing Managers
- Tenants

## Functional Requirements

FR1: Users shall be able to select a repair category.

FR2: Users shall be able to answer repair assessment questions.

FR3: The system shall assign a repair priority.

FR4: The system shall recommend an appropriate trade.

FR5: The system shall generate a unique reference number.

FR6: The system shall store assessment records.

FR7: Users shall be able to view historical assessments.

FR8: Users shall be able to search assessments by reference number.

FR9: Users shall be able to export assessment data to CSV.

FR10: Users shall be able to filter assessment history.

## Success Criteria

- Consistent repair categorisation.
- Improved reporting capability.
- Reduced manual assessment effort.
- Improved access to historical records.
